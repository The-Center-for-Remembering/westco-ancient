# 🖌️ Designer

**West Co.**

---

## Design how people measure a lifetime

Most designers spend their careers shaping parts of something bigger. But every so often, a chance comes along to create something entirely new — a new category, a new interface, a new way people understand themselves.

Tangle is one of those chances. It reimagines time not as hours to manage but as a living story to grow into. It's a tool for meaning, helping people plan with intention, cherish memories, and see the deeper threads of life.

The product is real. People are already using it. Now we're crossing the line where a soulful idea becomes something the world can't live without — and you'll do it alongside visionary technologists like [Evan Sharp and Biz Stone](https://www.wsj.com/opinion/faith-freedom-and-the-long-thread-of-technology-554b95f3), building something that will shape how people live for decades to come.

We're looking for a designer who cares deeply about what design can do. Someone ready to shape a category-defining product from its earliest stage and help millions live more intentional, meaningful lives.

---

## In this role, you will:

- **Invent a new lifetime interface:** Rethink how humans see and shape their lives, across linear, cyclical and narrative time, and design an experience that feels inevitable once you see it.
- **Shape product-market fit:** Work at the heart of the company, turning promising mechanics into beloved rituals. Rapidly test, refine and iterate to make the product indispensable.
- **Design the daily experience:** Create the features and flows that become part of people's emotional routines, from the way they plan to reflect, celebrate and grow.
- **Prototype the future**: Give form to ideas no one's built before, translating fuzzy concepts to elegant user experiences.
- **Ship the soul:** Partner closely with world-class engineers to bring your work into the world fast and relentlessly beautiful.

**And in the other 5% of your time:**

- **Evolve the brand**: Help define a visual identity that feels as timeless and human as the product itself.
- **Extend our reach**: Draw from a global network of designers and thinkers who can push our work forward.

---

## What we're looking for

**Your craft:**

- **Product design:** You have a deep product sense and know how to shape high-impact consumer experiences from the ground up.
- **Interaction design:** You simplify complex ideas into clear, intuitive, and elegant flows.
- **Motion design:** You use motion to guide understanding, express emotion, and make the product feel alive.
- **Visual design:** You bring taste and style to every detail, crafting interfaces that feel inevitable and human.
- **Typography:** You use type as a core design element to create voice, rhythm, and clarity.

**Your character:**

- **Soulful:** Purpose drives you. You care deeply about the emotional and philosophical impact of the product you build.
- **Masterful:** You obsess over detail and typography. Nothing is done until it's beautiful, intentional, and true.
- **Courageous:** You're willing to speak hard truths, and you approach people with vulnerability and care.
- **Comfortable with ambiguity:** You love the messy, magical process of discovery that turns complexity into simplicity.
- **Curious:** You actively seek new perspectives and change your mind when you learn something new.

## Why this role is once-in-a-career

Most design roles happen after the story's already written. The mechanics are fixed, the constraints are set, and your job is to make what exists a little better.

This isn't that.

This is that rare, once-in-a-decade window where the product is real but still malleable — where the most important decisions about what it is and can become still lie ahead. It's the chance to shape a new category of software, influence how millions of people relate to their time, and put your fingerprint on something that might outlive us all.
