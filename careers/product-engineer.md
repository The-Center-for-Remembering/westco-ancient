# 🎚️ Software Engineer – Product

**West Co.**

---

We believe life is meant to be savored. Time is the most precious thing in our life—indeed, it *is* our life, our lifetime. Yet there are almost no tools for helping us savor the time we are alive. This is an astounding opportunity to build a meaningful product, significant business, and the next big consumer platform—one that helps people live life more on purpose.

We are seeking a skilled and passionate product-oriented software engineer to help us build and ship our product. You will play a key role in the creation and advancement of experiences on our Charma platform—a unified creative planner, social calendar, journal and guide. This role requires a blend of technical expertise, intuitive product judgment, and a passion for serving the deeper needs of people's everyday lives.

## What We're Looking For

This role is focused on creating and refining core product experiences. To do this, it'll help if you've had experience with:

- Creative problem-solving focused on people's needs
- Comfortable with ambiguity, self-direction, and ownership
- Building and shipping products at internet startup-speed
- Scaling systems to meet user demand
- Discernment in balancing craft and velocity
- Partnering closely with design

The next few years will be highly inventive and iterative, with a goal of helping us find a product-market fit that aligns with our core values.

Our engineering team consists mostly of curious generalists, each with different strengths. We enjoy being a small, nimble team that can bring ideas to life quickly. As we grow, we're committed to keeping that small-team magic.

---

## Technical Stack

Our technical stack consists of modern technologies such as:

- Postgres database
- Golang backend
- Svelte & Sveltekit frontend (with mostly TypeScript and TailwindCSS)
- CapacitorJS with native iOS functionalities (Android on the way)
- Thoughtful usage of modern AI throughout
- Git, Docker, GCP

You don't need to have mastered all of these technologies, but to succeed you'll need to be comfortable with core computer science principles and have a general curiosity and desire to learn.

## Who You Are

Finally, who you are as a human being matters a lot to us. We're looking for a *soulful builder* who brings thoughtfulness and care to their work. Ideally you're a:

- Hands-on builder, eager to explore (and often discard) product ideas
- Self-directed problem-solver, with good instincts about where to focus
- Humanist technologist, called to create better conditions for human wellbeing
- Holistic builder, eager to invite in folks outside engineering to the product process
- Compassionate communicator, emanating maturity, self-awareness and care
- Curious learner, hungry to integrate new perspectives and evolve your opinions

## Work Environment

This role reports to Ben Finkel, who manages Engineering for West Co. and also helps lead product development. While some of our team works out of our headquarters in the Presidio of San Francisco, our engineering and design teams are fully remote. Our full company meets on Zoom every week and in person in San Francisco once per season.
