# Our Practices

> We've learned that **how we are together** becomes the **impact we have together**.

At West Co., we invest **3% of our work time** into intentional practices to foster **100% more team depth**.

These practices help us stay connected to each other, rooted in what matters, and in rhythm with the world around us.

---

## 🤲 Small Groups

We use **Small Groups** to deepen our connections.

Small groups create intimate spaces where team members can know and be known, building the trust and relationships that make great work possible.

[Learn about Small Groups →](small-groups.md)

---

## 🕯️ Ancestor Tables

We care for an **Ancestor Table** to stay rooted in what matters.

The ancestor table practice helps us remember and honor the wisdom that came before us, keeping us grounded in values larger than ourselves.

[Learn about Ancestor Tables →](ancestor-tables.md)

---

## ❄️ Working With the Seasons

We **Work With the Seasons** to stay in rhythm with the world.

By aligning our work patterns with natural cycles, we honor the rhythms of growth, harvest, rest, and renewal.

[Learn about Working With the Seasons →](working-with-seasons.md)

---

## 🛟 Practicing Covenant

We **Practice Covenant** to try our best to grow together.

Covenant practice means making commitments to each other and the work we do together, creating the foundation for long-term growth and trust.

[Learn about Practicing Covenant →](practicing-covenant.md)

---

## Why We Practice

These practices aren't just nice-to-haves. They're core to how we work. We believe that:

- The quality of our relationships shapes the quality of our work
- Wisdom traditions offer valuable insights for modern work
- Sustainable teams require intentional care and attention
- Small investments in culture create outsized returns

**3% of our time. 100% more depth.**
